## Title

Respiración Pro

## Short description

Offline guided breathing for calm, focus, and mindful breaks.

## Full description

Respiración Pro helps you take guided breathing breaks with a calm interface, no accounts, no ads, and no required internet connection.

Choose routines such as 4-7-8, box breathing, calm mode, free mode, or create your own custom pattern. The app shows the rhythm on screen, can guide you with sound, voice, and vibration, and stores your stats only on your device.

Main features:

- 4-7-8, box, calm, energize, free, and custom routines
- Ambient sounds generated on-device
- Voice guidance in Spanish and English
- Local daily statistics
- Data export and import
- Light and dark themes
- Offline use
- No ads, analytics, account, or trackers

Respiración Pro is a general wellbeing tool. It does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
